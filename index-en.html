Japanese Fill-In Technology

✦ What is the Japanese Fill-In Method?

Unlike conventional gel removals that rely on repeated harsh acetone soaks and surface re-buffing, our Japanese Fill-In method only gently files away the top color layer while keeping the original bonded base gel intact. This protects your natural nails from thinning and brittleness.

  • ✓ Zero Over-Filing: Preserves natural nail thickness and strength
  • ✓ Acetone-Free Care: Prevents chemical dryness on cuticles and skin
  • ✓ Precision & Efficiency: Masterful speed honed in top-tier Tokyo salons
